## Game data and icons

The quest list, Arena loaner sets and labels in [docs/data.js](docs/data.js), the monster,
quest-type and weapon-class icons in [docs/assets/MonsterIcons/](docs/assets/MonsterIcons/) and
[docs/assets/WeaponIcons/](docs/assets/WeaponIcons/), and the Guild Card backgrounds in
[docs/assets/GuildCardBG/](docs/assets/GuildCardBG/), are generated by
[scripts/build_data.py](scripts/build_data.py) from a copy of the game extracted by the author from a
personally owned cartridge. **No game files, archives or keys are redistributed**; only the generated
data, icons and images are committed.

Some Guild Card backgrounds are the game's collaboration art and carry other companies' marks as
the game ships them (among them Nintendo, SEGA, Square Enix, BANDAI NAMCO, Universal Studios Japan
and Uniqlo). Those marks and characters belong to their owners.
