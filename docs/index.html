How to use

1. Pick a quest type & level. Choose Caravan, Gathering Hall, Arena or ALL, then a From / Up to Level range. In ALL the range runs Caravan 1★ → Gathering Hall G3★ → Arena G3★.

2. Narrow the pool with the sidebar filters:

  • Quest Filters — uncheck a category to drop every quest in it. Capture, Multi Monster, Repel, Frenzy and Apex sit under Large Monsters and go with it. Keys Only? keeps just the quests the game marks as Key. Quest Type Filters switch individual stars off everywhere (Arena is off to start with).
  • Monsters — uncheck a monster to drop every quest that targets it.
  • Weapons — uncheck a weapon class to stop it being rolled.
  • Challenges — add your own conditions (e.g. "No Armor"), each with a chance %, that roll alongside the quest. Set how many can apply per quest, and toggle each one on/off. Chaos Mode hands the on/off decision to the RNG each roll.

3. Hit Randomize. You get a quest and a weapon class, plus any challenges that rolled. Arena quests give you one of the quest's five loaner sets instead, since that's what you fight in.

Quest badges: Urgent Key Hunt Capture Repel Small Monsters Delivery Harvest Tour Frenzy Apex Arena

⚙ Theme — change the color theme, put one of the game's Guild Card backgrounds behind the card, or a flat blue/green for chroma-keying.

↗ Stream view — pops the result card out into its own window on a green background, updating with every roll, for capturing in OBS.

Export / Import — save your filters and challenges to a file, or load them back.

Filters, challenges and theme are saved between visits. Reset Filters restores the defaults and keeps your challenges.

Reset Filters?

Quest filters, levels, monsters and weapons go back to their defaults. Your challenges are kept.

MH4U Quest Randomizer

An unofficial, non-commercial quest randomizer for Monster Hunter 4 Ultimate — it rolls a quest and a weapon for when you can't decide what to hunt. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or supported by Capcom.

Every quest — its name, star, locale, objective and monsters — and every Arena loaner set is read from the game's own quest files, and the monster, quest and weapon icons and the Guild Card backgrounds are the game's own (the collaboration backgrounds' marks belong to their owners). Everything was extracted from a personally owned copy of the game; no game files are redistributed. Downloadable Event and Challenge Quests aren't on the cartridge, so they aren't in the pool, and Guild Quests are built by the game at run time, so they aren't either. The monster classes in the Monsters filter are assigned by hand, under the game's own class names.

Monster Hunter 4 Ultimate and all of its names, data and icons are © CAPCOM CO., LTD. The UI textures and the MHFU font are shared with the same author's MHGU fan apps, and the app itself is a port of the MH3U Quest Randomizer, itself a port of the MHGU Quest Randomizer.

Built with substantial help from Claude Code, Anthropic's AI coding tool, directed and reviewed by the author.
